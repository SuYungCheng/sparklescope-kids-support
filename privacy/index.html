「亮晶晶探索隊」（SparkleScope Kids）由獨立開發者 SuYungCheng 提供。我們重視兒童與家庭的隱私。

我們不收集的資料

本 App 不建立使用者帳號，不收集、儲存或傳送姓名、電子郵件、位置、通訊錄、廣告識別碼、使用分析、診斷資料或其他個人資料。本 App 不含第三方廣告或第三方分析服務。

相機

相機用於即時掃描互動與裝置上的手部追蹤效果。相機畫面僅在裝置上用於當下互動；App 不會拍照、錄影、上傳或儲存相機影像。

儲存在裝置上的偏好設定

語言、音效、語音、牙齒階段、蟲蟲樣式與完整版解鎖狀態儲存在使用者的裝置上，用來提供功能與記住選擇。開發者不會將這些資料上傳到伺服器。

應用程式內購買

購買由 Apple 與 App Store 處理。開發者不收集或處理付款卡資料。

兒童隱私

本 App 不收集兒童的個人資料，不含廣告或社群功能。購買與外部連結位於家長驗證之後。

資料保留、刪除與選擇

因開發者不收集或持有使用者資料，沒有伺服器端資料需要保留或刪除。使用者可刪除 App 以移除裝置上的偏好設定，並可隨時在 iPhone 設定中取消相機權限。

聯絡與政策變更

如政策更新，本頁會更新「最後更新」日期。若有隱私問題，請透過 專案支援頁面 聯絡 SuYungCheng；請勿在公開 issue 中附上兒童照片或個人資料。

English

SparkleScope Kids is provided by SuYungCheng, an independent developer. We value the privacy of children and families.

Data we do not collect

The app does not create user accounts or collect, store, or transmit names, email addresses, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, analytics, diagnostics, or other personal data. It contains no third-party advertising or third-party analytics services.

Camera

The camera is used for the live scanning experience and on-device hand-tracking effects. Camera frames are used on the device for the current interaction only. The app does not take photos, record video, upload images, or save camera frames.

Preferences stored on the device

Language, sound, voice, teeth stage, bug style, and Full Version entitlement state are stored locally to provide features and remember choices. The developer does not upload this information to a server.

In-App Purchase

Purchases are processed by Apple and the App Store. The developer does not collect or process payment-card information.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from children and contains no advertising or social features. Purchase opportunities and external links are placed behind a parental gate.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Because the developer does not collect or hold user data, there is no server-side user data to retain or delete. Users can delete the app to remove local preferences and revoke camera permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

Contact and changes

If this policy changes, the updated date will appear on this page. For privacy questions, contact SuYungCheng through the project support page. Do not post a child's photo or personal information in a public issue.
