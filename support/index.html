相機無法使用

請前往 iPhone「設定」→「隱私與安全性」→「相機」，確認已允許「亮晶晶探索隊」使用相機。App 不會拍照或儲存相機影像。

恢復完整版

在首頁點選任一上鎖功能，通過家長驗證後進入完整版畫面，點選「恢復購買」。請使用原先購買的 Apple Account。

聯絡支援

請在 GitHub 開啟一筆支援請求，開發者 SuYungCheng 會於該頁回覆。

開啟支援請求

請附上 App 版本、iOS 版本、iPhone 機型與問題步驟。請勿張貼孩子照片、Apple Account、購買憑證或其他個人資料。

English

Camera unavailable

Open iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and make sure SparkleScope Kids is allowed to use the camera. The app does not take photos or save camera images.

Restore the Full Version

On the Home screen, tap any locked feature. Complete the parental gate, then tap Restore Purchases on the Full Version screen. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Contact support

Open a GitHub support request to contact the independent developer, SuYungCheng.

Open a support request

Include the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, and steps that reproduce the issue. Do not post a child's photo, Apple Account, purchase receipt, or other personal information.
